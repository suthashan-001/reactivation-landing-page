Frequently asked questions

Is this just a newsletter?

No. Newsletters are broad updates for active patients. IntakeAI targets patients who went quiet and drafts messages based on their last visit, concern, and likely reason to return.

Do messages send automatically?

No. The clinician reviews, edits, approves, or skips messages before anything sends.

Do you integrate directly with Jane App?

The first pilot can run from a simple CSV export. Direct integrations can be explored later, but the first goal is to prove reactivation demand without overbuilding.

Can we use anonymized data first?

Yes. Demo workflows can use anonymized or sample data before live patient data is involved.

Can you guarantee 15 patients?

No. Patient response depends on list size, relationship quality, timing, and clinic reputation. The 15-patient target comes from a conservative 5% planning estimate on a 300-patient inactive list.

What happens after I book?

We review your clinic size, inactive patient list, current follow-up process, and whether a pilot makes sense. If there is a fit, we map the CSV export and first reactivation cohort.

Is this for all healthcare clinics?

The first version is focused specifically on naturopathic clinics because the messaging needs to understand naturopathic follow-up patterns.
